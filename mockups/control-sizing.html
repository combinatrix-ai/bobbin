Control sizing: unify to one grid

Today the popover mixes four icon-button standards (18×16, 24×24, 25×25, 28×28) and its most-used action is the smallest. Proposal: one standard — icon buttons 28×28 with 12pt glyphs, send 32×32, rewrite buttons 24×20, meta-line text 9.5 → 10.5. Dashed outlines show the actual hit target.
